What changes

The top plate lists where you're going now, where you'll hand in and what comes next, the way trail signs stack destinations. The stripe on the board grades the step: plain yellow for easy ground, red and white when mobs come in groups. The climb at the bottom is your progress through the guide.

Trade-off. Sign yellow is loud. It needs a dimmer setting for night play.

Building it in WoW

Each plate is a stretchable body plus a pointed end cap, so long names fit. The climb is drawn with the Line API from the guide's step count.

Effort: moderate.

3

Transit

A guide is one route with errands running alongside it. Draw it as a transit map: each quest is a coloured line, and it ends where you hand it in.

84 yd to Defias fields
Westfall
Levels 13–15
Step 12 of 64
Each colour is a quest. A line ends where you hand it in.

What changes

Side objectives stop being a separate list. They ride alongside the route as coloured lines until the station where you hand them in, so you can see what's still open and where it ends. The light panel reads the same over snow, swamp or a dungeon floor.

Trade-off. A light panel is the brightest thing on a night screen. It needs an opacity setting, and long guides need the map to scroll.

Palette

  • Map white#F6F7F5
  • Ink#1B1F24
  • Route gold#F0B000
  • Militia red#DC241F

Type

Defias fields Defias Trapper 9/15

Schibsted Grotesk. Heavy weight for station names, regular for the rest, like a printed line map.

Building it in WoW

Guides already mark parallel objectives with stickystart and stickystop, so the lines come from existing data. Lines, ticks and stations are plain textures.

Effort: moderate to high. The layout logic is the work.

What changes

The step number becomes the card's rank and each kind of step has a suit: kill, collect, talk or travel. Kill counts are pips, like the pips on a playing card. The corner of the next card tells you what's coming. Press Play card to bring it forward.

Trade-off. One step in detail at a time. Players who like to read ahead see less.

Counts move onto mechanical tally drums that roll over with each kill; press Kill a Defias Trapper at the top to hear them in your head. The radar shows the target and the next two stops together, so you always know what's after this.

What changes

Only the current objective is on screen. The direction is a warm glow at the screen edge with the distance beside it. The full list slides in from the left while you hold Alt.

Halo

Put the arrow where your eyes already are. A ring at your character's feet points the way, and meters beside you fill as you go.

Westfall 13–15Step 12 of 64

What changes

In a fight you watch your character, not the corner of the screen. The ring at your feet points the way and the meters beside you fill as you go, with text cut down to numbers.

Trade-off. It assumes the camera centres your character. Players who zoom far out or offset the camera will need to move it.

Palette

  • Ring#F7F5F0
  • Meter gold#FFC83D
  • Track, 28%#FFFFFF
  • Shadow, 60%#000000

Type

9/15 Trappers

Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for readers with low vision. Its distinct letterforms hold up when you glance down mid-fight.

Building it in WoW

Meters are Cooldown swipes, which the game draws natively. The ring is a fixed overlay at screen centre with a user offset; the notch is a rotated texture.

Effort: moderate.

The progress bars are the feature. A lit channel is easy to catch from the corner of your eye, even in dark zones, and a finished objective cools into gold inlay. Kill a Defias Trapper to watch the channel fill.

The guide is cloth hanging from the top of the screen. Finished steps are stitched off and the current one is outlined in gold thread. Colours follow your faction, and the crest is GoatQuest's own goat.

Starchart

Read the route the way Sentinels read the sky. Every stop in the guide is a star, placed from its real map coordinates.

84 yd

The People's Militia Step 12 of 64

Defias Trapper9/15
Defias Smuggler11/15

What changes

The whole route is visible at once. Where you've been burns bright and the dotted line is what's left. It's the one direction here in cool colours, so it stands apart from the game's gold UI.

Trade-off. The chart shows where, not what. It always needs the text beneath it.

Palette

  • Night#10163A
  • Starlight#E6ECFF
  • Silver#A9B4D6
  • Violet#9C8CFF
  • Moonwell#6FE3D6

Type

Defias fields Defias Trapper 9/15

Josefin Sans throughout. Its geometric, slightly old-world shapes suit the chart without needing a display face.

Building it in WoW

Star positions come straight from each step's goto coordinates. Stars and lines are textures inside a circular mask.

Effort: moderate.
